Part D – Product Thinking

If I owned this rewards and redemption product, I would improve these three things:

1. Simple and User-Friendly Interface

Make it easy for customers to check their points balance, transaction history, and available rewards. This improves user experience and encourages customers to use the product regularly.

2. Points Expiry Notifications

Send SMS, WhatsApp, or app notifications before reward points expire. This helps customers use their points on time and improves customer satisfaction.

3. Business Analytics Dashboard

Provide store owners with a dashboard showing total points earned, redeemed, expired, and customer activity. This helps businesses understand customer behavior and make better decisions about reward offers.

Conclusion: These improvements would make the product easier to use, increase customer engagement, and help businesses improve customer retention.
